# Второй раунд — промпты

Создано встроенным image_gen с визуальными референсами из пяти кейсов, предоставленных пользователем. Первый раунд отклонён как слишком классический. Новые направления: современный гротеск, оптическое стекло, объёмные объекты, цвет и пространственная композиция. Это дизайн-концепции, не снимки реализованного приложения.

## 04-volta

Референсы: /tmp/tetr-design-refs/volta-06.webp, /tmp/tetr-design-refs/volta-08.webp

Use case: ui-mockup. Create ONE new, extraordinarily crafted iOS dashboard screen for Tetrin, a car-wash business owner app, in Russian. The user rejected previous concepts as too classical and supplied these exact visual references. Use attached images strictly as visual STYLE REFERENCES; do NOT reproduce their brands, energy metrics, bank functions, legal documents or marketing presentation. The requested result is a complete single practical phone screen, NOT a Behance presentation collage. Front-on orthographic slim iPhone frame, same device and scale, full entire phone occupying 92% of canvas height, a neutral light gray #E6E7E8 external backdrop and small equal margins, no hands, no perspective, no text outside device. Render high resolution portrait. All typography must be modern geometric SANS-SERIF like Urbanist/SF Pro/Manrope, absolutely NO SERIF, NO classic book or luxury magazine typography, no beige-burgundy styling. Keep safe areas/status bar/Dynamic Island and bottom home indicator. Main page title "Сводка", small Tetrin wordmark and bell button. Local period selector "Сегодня", "Месяц", "Прошлый" with first selected. Accurate daily sample data: net income "23 000 ֏", label "Чистый доход", gross "31 000 ֏" label "Поступления", payroll "8 000 ֏" label "Сотрудникам", expense "0 ֏" label "Расходы", service count "6 машин", team "2 на смене". These amounts must all appear and agree (31000-8000-0=23000). Four-tab native bottom nav "Смена", "Сводка" selected, "Зарплата", "Ещё". Legible 16pt-equivalent Russian body text, readable large numbers, purposeful depth and custom icon craft, no random filler, no made-up growth percentages, no goals or irrelevant controls. Design the actual app screen with truly creative composition and rich material, not just a boring wireframe recolor. DIRECTION 01 "Pulse": transfer the source VOLTA's lit-from-within optical glass materials, luminous cyan rim, amber and lime instrument accents, Doto dot-matrix numeric typography and dark rich cyan atmosphere into a bespoke financial dashboard. This should feel like a beautifully tangible advanced digital control surface. Screen background near-black petrol #080F15. Layout: compact heading and period row at top; one wide hero glass module with a finely illuminated cyan upper left rim and rounded 28pt corners, title Чистый доход, enormous luminous WHITE dot-matrix "23 000" and smaller regular sans "֏". A small inset 3D translucent cyan folded receipt icon at the top right of this module. A beautifully designed accounting flow in bottom part of hero: "31 000" amber node labeled "Поступления", flowing via dotted line to "8 000" warm white node labeled "Сотрудникам" and "0" neutral node labeled "Расходы". Every label and number readable, not scientific jargon. Below hero, asymmetric two-widget row: a slightly wider lime optical glass tile with giant dotted "6", small "машин", a tasteful pale lime 3D miniature side-view car partially emerging at upper right; smaller frosted charcoal tile "2" "на смене" and two letter avatars К/А. Then a matte dark activity panel with title "Последние машины" and small "Все →"; two rows "77 AA 777" "Кузов · 16:39" "3 000 ֏", "99 BB 888" "Химчистка · 16:27" "12 000 ֏", compact clear type and small custom car silhouettes, NOT classic table rows filling screen. Bottom dock smoked glass with bright cyan selected tab. Cyan, amber and lime appear on functional modules, consistent with provided VOLTA source. Glow originates at glass edges and diffuses gently inward; never a crude neon outline. Bold creative physicality, refined microcontrast, no giant circular donut chart.
